Portrait last-reply bounds and cursor/stop bounds passed at 100%/150% fonts; landscape cursor/stop bounds passed at 150%. This is emulator QA, not a physical-phone or iOS claim.

- Public-source/history and known-local-secret release scanners must pass before publication. Their coverage is detection of known values/patterns, not a guarantee of zero unknown secrets or legal clearance. File-only encryption retains its existing lack of forward secrecy; chat transport is not described as full-app E2EE.
